# Etsubdink Legesse — Ethiopian Traditional Painting

Generate illustrations that faithfully capture the specific visual language of **Etsubdink Legesse**, Ethiopia's master of traditional flat narrative painting. His work continues the tradition of Ethiopian Orthodox church murals and illuminated Ge'ez manuscripts, rendered with a contemporary sensibility. The output should feel handcrafted, symbolic, narrative, and timeless — flat 2D, bold outlines, warm earth tones, and large expressive eyes.

## About Etsubdink Legesse

Etsubdink Legesse is a contemporary Ethiopian artist working within the tradition of Ethiopian Orthodox manuscript illumination and church mural painting. His distinctive style features:
- **Narrative panel compositions** — multiple scenes arranged in grids with individual borders
- **Ge'ez/Amharic manuscript text** as decorative titles and inscriptions
- **Red/terracotta geometric borders** framing each panel
- **Flat 2D perspective** — no depth, no foreshortening, no realistic anatomy
- **Bold black outlines** defining every form
- **Flat unshaded colors** — warm earth tones, no gradients
- **Large almond-shaped eyes** — the most defining facial feature
- **Simplified rounded figures** in traditional Ethiopian garments

**IMPORTANT**: This is NOT the same as the Afewerk Tekle style. Legesse's work is flat, 2D, and manuscript-like. Tekle's work is volumetric, chiaroscuro, and oil painting. Never confuse the two.

## Style Characteristics

These non-negotiable traits define Etsubdink Legesse's painting style. Every generated image must exhibit all of them:

### Composition
- **Flat two-dimensional composition** — no realistic perspective, depth, or foreshortening
- **Front-facing or simple profile** figures — never three-quarter view
- **Balanced, evenly spaced** subjects with similar head heights when grouped
- **Narrative clarity** — the story must be instantly readable
- **Red/terracotta geometric border** — Legesse's signature framing device
- **Multi-panel layouts** — multiple narrative scenes arranged in grids

### Figure Style
- **Large almond-shaped expressive eyes** — the most defining feature
- **Simplified noses and mouths** — small, symbolic, not detailed
- **Warm brown skin tones** — consistent across all figures
- **Rounded simplified anatomy** — no muscular definition, no realistic proportions
- **Decorative patterned clothing** — Ethiopian textiles with geometric motifs
- **Traditional Ethiopian garments** whenever appropriate (habesha kemis, netela, gabi)

### Color
- **Flat unshaded color only** — no gradients, no shadows, no highlights
- **Warm earthy palette**: deep red, terracotta, golden yellow, ochre, forest green, muted blue, black, white
- **Warm ivory/cream background** — never white, never dark
- **No realistic lighting** — no directional light, no reflections, no cinematic effects

### Details
- **Bold clean black outlines** around every form
- **Ge'ez or Amharic manuscript-like text** as decorative elements near top or bottom
- **Stylized natural elements**: trees with rounded canopies, simplified plants, basic architecture
- **Traditional objects**: Ethiopian crosses, ceremonial umbrellas, jebena (coffee pots), mesob (woven baskets), masinqo (fiddle), kebero (drum)

## What to Avoid

Never generate any of the following:
- Photorealism or photographic quality
- 3D rendering or volumetric lighting
- Gradients, shadows, or highlights
- Realistic anatomy or proportions
- Western perspective or depth
- Soft digital painting or airbrush effects
- Anime, manga, or western comic styles
- Glossy materials or reflections
- Modern illustration aesthetics
- Painterly brush strokes or texture
- Dark or moody atmospheres
- Cinematic color grading

The result must immediately evoke Ethiopian manuscript and mural painting while adapting naturally to any requested subject.

## Composition Rules

### Single Figure (Portrait)
- Figure centered or slightly off-center
- Upper body visible, hands often shown in gesture
- Simple background: solid color, single tree, or architectural element
- Decorative border framing

### Group Scene
- Figures arranged in rows, evenly spaced
- Similar head heights — no dramatic size differences
- Central figure slightly larger if hierarchy is needed
- Background elements distributed symmetrically

### Narrative Scene
- Multiple zones within the frame telling a story
- Left-to-right or top-to-bottom reading order
- Clear action and reaction between figures
- Objects and attributes identifying participants

### Religious/Sacred
- Halo or nimbus around holy figures
- Ethiopian cross motifs in borders and on garments
- Ceremonial umbrellas over important figures
- Ge'ez text as inscriptions

## Agent Execution Protocol

When a user requests an image in this style:

1. **Identify the subject** — portrait, group, narrative, modern, religious, landscape
2. **Determine the composition** — single figure, balanced group, or multi-zone narrative
3. **Select appropriate details** — garments, objects, architectural elements
4. **Build the prompt** using the template above
5. **Validate** against the checklist below
6. **Present** the prompt ready for use with any AI image generator

### Validation Checklist

Before presenting the prompt, verify:

- [ ] Flat 2D composition specified (no perspective/depth)
- [ ] Bold black outlines mentioned
- [ ] Flat unshaded colors (no gradients/shadows)
- [ ] Large almond-shaped eyes described
- [ ] Warm brown skin tones included
- [ ] Traditional Ethiopian garments referenced
- [ ] Warm ivory/cream background specified
- [ ] Geometric border mentioned
- [ ] No forbidden elements (photorealism, 3D, anime, etc.)
- [ ] Subject is clearly described with narrative intent

## Integration Notes

This skill works with any AI image generator:
- **Midjourney**: Append `--ar 1:1 --s 250 --q 2` (square format matches panel tradition)
- **DALL-E / ChatGPT**: Use the prompt as-is
- **Stable Diffusion**: Add negative prompt: `"photorealistic, 3D, gradients, shadows, anime, comic, western illustration, glossy, modern digital art"`
- **Flux**: Use the prompt as-is, specify square format
- **generate_image tool**: Paste prompt directly

For multi-panel compositions (like the reference), generate each panel separately, then arrange in a grid with consistent borders.
