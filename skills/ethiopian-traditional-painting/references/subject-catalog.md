# Subject Catalog — Ethiopian Traditional Painting

## How to Render Specific Elements

This catalog describes how to translate any object, garment, or element into the Ethiopian painting visual language.

---

## Human Figures

### Face
- **Shape**: Rounded rectangle or oval
- **Eyes**: Large almond-shaped, solid dark iris, visible white on both sides
- **Nose**: Simple curved line or small triangle, minimal
- **Mouth**: Small line or slight curve, subordinate to eyes
- **Ears**: Simple curved lines or omitted
- **Expression**: Neutral to slight smile, conveyed through eyes

### Hair
- **Style**: Short, rounded, solid black
- **Texture**: Indicated by outline shape, not individual strands
- **Beard**: Rounded chin shape, not individual hairs
- **Head covering**: Simple draped form

### Hands
- **Style**: Simplified, mitten-like
- **Fingers**: Parallel lines or not distinguished
- **Gestures**: Raised (blessing), holding (objects), at sides (neutral)
- **Never**: Realistic finger detail, nails, or knuckles

### Feet
- **Style**: Simple ovals or hidden by garments
- **Footwear**: Simple sandals as flat shapes
- **Never**: Detailed toes, laces, or complex shoes

## Garments

### Habesha Kemis (Traditional Dress)
```
┌──────────────────┐
│ ╔══════════════╗ │  ← Colorful embroidered neckline
│ ║              ║ │
│ ║   WHITE      ║ │  ← White cotton body
│ ║   COTTON     ║ │
│ ║              ║ │
│ ╠══════════════╣ │  ← Colorful embroidered waistband
│ ║              ║ │
│ ║   WHITE      ║ │
│ ║   SKIRT      ║ │
│ ╠══════════════╣ │  ← Colorful embroidered hem border
└──────────────────┘
```
- Base: Pure white
- Borders: Deep red, golden ochre, forest green geometric patterns
- Patterns: Diamonds, zigzags, crosses, circles
- Fit: Loose, simple draping, no complex folds

### Netela (Shawl)
```
     ┌─────────┐
    ╱  BORDER   ╲
   ╱   WHITE     ╲
  ╱    SHAWL      ╲
 ╱─────────────────╲
```
- Base: White or cream
- Border: Colorful geometric pattern along edges
- Drape: Over shoulders, may cover head
- Length: Reaches waist or below

### Gabi (Heavy Shawl)
- Thicker than netela
- Often plain white or cream
- Wrapped around body for warmth
- Simple, bulky form

### Clergy Vestments
- Elaborate embroidered robes
- Cross patterns throughout
- More colorful than lay garments
- May include cape-like elements

### Royal/Elite Garments
- Rich golden and red colors
- Elaborate geometric patterns
- Crown or headpiece
- Ceremonial accessories

## Objects

### Ethiopian Cross
- **Style**: Geometric, interlocking designs
- **Varieties**: Lalibela, Gondar, Axumite, processional
- **Shape**: Never a simple Latin cross
- **Usage**: On garments, borders, held by clergy, architectural detail
- **Colors**: Golden ochre, deep red, or black outline only

### Jebena (Coffee Pot)
```
    ┌─┐
    │ │  ← Spout
  ┌─┴─┴─┐
  │     │  ← Round body
  │     │
  └──┬──┘
     │    ← Base
```
- Shape: Round body, narrow neck, curved spout
- Material: Black or dark brown (clay)
- Usage: Coffee ceremonies, domestic scenes

### Mesob (Woven Basket)
```
   ╱╲
  ╱  ╲  ← Conical lid
 ╱    ╲
┌──────┐
│      │  ← Woven body
│      │
└──────┘
```
- Shape: Conical lid on round body
- Material: Woven grass, colorful patterns
- Usage: Serving food (injera), market scenes

### Masinqo (Fiddle)
```
  ┌─┐
  │ │  ← Neck
  │ │
 ┌┴─┴┐
 │   │  ← Resonator (covered with skin)
 └───┘
```
- Shape: One-stringed fiddle
- Material: Wooden neck, skin-covered resonator
- Usage: Music, celebration, courtship

### Kebero (Drum)
```
  ┌─────┐
  │     │  ← Skin head
  │     │
  │     │  ← Wooden body
  │     │
  └─────┘
```
- Shape: Conical or cylindrical
- Material: Wooden body, animal skin head
- Usage: Church music, celebration

### Ceremonial Umbrella
```
     ╱╲
    ╱  ╲
   ╱    ╲  ← Concentric color rings
  ╱──────╲
  │      │  ← Tassel/fringe
  │  │   │  ← Pole
  │  │   │
```
- Shape: Domed top on pole
- Colors: Concentric rings of bright colors
- Usage: Over clergy, royalty, sacred objects
- Symbol: Protection, honor

### Injera (Flatbread)
- Shape: Large circular flatbread
- Color: Light brown with spongy texture
- Served: On mesob, shared communally
- Depiction: Simple circle, no texture detail

### Coffee Beans/Cups
- Beans: Small oval shapes in pile
- Cups: Small handleless cups (cini)
- Roasting: Smoke rising symbolically

## Nature

### Trees
```
    ╱╲╱╲
   ╱╲╱╲╱╲  ← Rounded canopy (circles or cloud shapes)
  ╱╲╱╲╱╲╱╲
     ││     ← Straight trunk
     ││
```
- Canopy: Rounded, cloud-like, or circular clusters
- Trunk: Straight, simple, brown
- Varieties: Acacia (flat top), palm (frond crown), fig (round)
- Style: Always simplified, never detailed bark or leaves

### Mountains/Hills
- Shape: Simple triangular or curved forms
- Color: Green (fertile) or brown (arid)
- Placement: Background, horizon line
- Never: Detailed rock formations

### Plants/Grass
- Style: Simple vertical lines or small shapes
- Grouping: Clusters, not individual plants
- Color: Green on brown ground
- Never: Detailed botanical accuracy

### Water
- Style: Simple horizontal lines or wavy lines
- Color: Muted blue
- Usage: Rivers, lakes, ceremonial washing
- Never: Realistic waves, reflections, or transparency

## Architecture

### Tukul (Round Hut)
```
     ╱╲
    ╱  ╲  ← Conical thatched roof
   ╱    ╲
  ╱──────╲
  │      │  ← Round wall
  │  ┌┐  │  ← Door
  └──┘┘──┘
```
- Shape: Round body, conical roof
- Material: Mud/stone walls, thatched roof
- Detail: Simple door, small window

### Church (Round Style)
```
     ╱╲
    ╱  ╲  ← Conical roof with cross on top
   ╱    ╲
  ╱──────╲
  │      │  ← Round body (larger than tukul)
  │  ┌┐  │  ← Door
  └──┘┘──┘
  +
  Ethiopian cross at apex
```
- Shape: Similar to tukul but larger
- Distinguishing feature: Cross on roof peak
- Often surrounded by courtyard

### Rectangular House
- Shape: Simple rectangular form
- Roof: Flat or slightly pitched
- Detail: Door, windows as simple rectangles
- Color: Terracotta or brown walls

### Castle/Fort (Gondar Style)
- Shape: Crenellated walls, towers
- Detail: Arched windows, decorative elements
- Color: Stone gray or brown
- Usage: Royal or historical scenes

## Decorative Elements

### Geometric Border Patterns
- **Zigzag**: Continuous W or M pattern
- **Diamond**: Repeating diamond shapes
- **Cross**: Small cross motifs repeated
- **Circle**: Chain of circles
- **Spiral**: Continuous spiral line
- **Wave**: Continuous S-curve

### Ge'ez/Amharic Text
- Placement: Top or bottom of panel
- Style: Decorative, not necessarily readable
- Function: Title, label, or ornamental
- Color: Black or dark brown

### Halos/Nimbuses
- Shape: Circular, behind head
- Color: Golden ochre
- Size: Slightly larger than head
- Usage: Holy figures, saints, clergy

### Clouds
- Style: Simple curved lines or stacked semicircles
- Color: Warm ivory (lighter than sky)
- Usage: Divine presence, sky decoration
- Never: Realistic cumulus or atmospheric effects
