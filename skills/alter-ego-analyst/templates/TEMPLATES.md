## Pillar IV: The Linguistic Shift

### Forbidden Vocabulary
Words and phrases the Core Self uses that the Alter Ego **bans:**
- "I think..." → Replace with "I know..." or "The data shows..."
- "I'll try..." → Replace with "I will..." or "My plan is..."
- "Sorry to bother you..." → Replace with direct, confident opening
- "Does that make sense?" → Replace with "What questions do you have?"
- "I'm just..." → Eliminate entirely

### Command Vocabulary
Power phrases native to the persona:
- "Here's the play..."
- "The path forward is clear."
- "I am here to [purpose], and this is how we do it."
- "What does success look like here?"
- "Let's execute."

- **The Threshold:** [Physical or mental barrier that marks the boundary]

1. **Acknowledge the boundary:** "The game is over. The meeting is done."
2. **Remove the totem:** [Physical action]
3. **Physical reset:** [Change posture, breathe, stretch]
4. **Gratitude practice:** "Thank you, [Alter Ego]. I've got it from here."
5. **Return to Core Self:** [Grounding activity]

### Five Bridges to Progress
