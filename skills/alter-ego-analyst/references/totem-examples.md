# Totem Examples Catalog

Common totems and artifacts serve as physical "switches" that signal your mind to activate your Alter Ego. These objects are generally categorized into three main types: something you wear, something you carry, or something connected to your environment.

## 1. Something You Wear (Wearables)

This is considered the most potent category because you can physically put the item on or take it off to "reset" your identity.

### Eyeglasses
- **Martin Luther King Jr.** — Used to look "distinguished"
- **Todd Herman** — Used prescription-free frames to activate his "Richard" persona
- **Function:** Symbolizes intelligence, focus, authority

### Hats and Headwear
- **Winston Churchill** — Famously used a wall of hats to choose which "self" to be for the day
- **Army Colonel** — Used a **baseball cap** to switch into his "Dad Self"
- **Function:** Signals a shift in identity or role

### Jewelry
- **Professional Executive** — Wore **Superman cufflinks** hidden under his sleeves
- **Equestrian** — Wore a **Wonder Woman bracelet**
- **General Examples:** Rings, necklaces, watches, lockets
- **Function:** Carries symbolic power close to the body

### Footwear
- **Beyoncé** — Used high heels as a trigger for Sasha Fierce
- **Businessman** — Used **Bruno Magli shoes**
- **Soccer Player** — Used **special insoles** with a stealth bomber sticker
- **Function:** Grounds the identity in physical sensation

### Specific Clothing
- **Professional** — An **old college hoodie** for confidence
- **Executive** — A **black tie** (representing Batman)
- **Athlete** — **Tiger-striped socks**
- **Medical Professional** — A **white lab coat** or uniform
- **Function:** Signals belonging to a specific role or team

## 2. Something You Carry

These are objects you can touch or hold during "Moments of Impact" to ground yourself in your hero's traits.

### Natural Objects
- **Baseball Player (Tony)** — Carried a **small pebble** from his family farm in his pocket
- **Function:** Connects to origin story and roots

### Tools of the Trade
- **Executive** — A **special pen** for signing important deals
- **Writer** — A specific **notebook** for creative work
- **General** — A unique **cup or mug** for focus work
- **Function:** Signals "work mode" and professionalism

### Sentimental Artifacts
- **John** — Used his grandfather's **pocket watch**
- **Mitch** — Placed an **old photo** of his grandmother on his desk to "switch on" her strength
- **Function:** Connects to legacy and inherited strength

### Triggers for Athletes
- **Todd Herman** — Slipped Walter Payton and Ronnie Lott **trading cards** into his uniform
- **Anthony (Basketball)** — Used a **towel** to imagine pulling on a "panther mask"
- **Function:** Embodies the spirit of heroes and competitors

## 3. Something Connected to the Field of Play

Sometimes the environment itself or a specific action within it acts as the totem.

### The Field Itself
- **Bo Jackson** — Used the football field as his trigger; the transformation into "Jason" began the moment he stepped onto the turf
- **Function:** The boundary between worlds is the totem

### Environmental Triggers
- **Executive** — A specific **writing chair**
- **Salesperson** — A specific **boardroom table**
- **Manager** — The **entryway to an office**
- **Function:** The physical space becomes the persona

### Charging Stations
- **Badminton Player** — Used a **folded wet towel** on the side of the court as a "charging station"
- **Function:** A place to "refuel" the Alter Ego's energy during breaks

## 4. The Alter Ego X Pill

A unique technique involves using a simple piece of **candy (like Tic Tacs)** as a placebo.

### The Ritual:
1. Hold the candy
2. Imagine it contains your Alter Ego's superpowers
3. Swallow it with intent
4. Activation complete

### Why It Works:
- **Placebo effect** triggers real internal shift
- **Inside-out effect** feels like awakening a hidden power
- **Universal availability** — can be used anywhere
