# Diagnostic Phase: Unmasking the Enemy

Before a hero can be built, the "Enemy" that keeps the individual in the **Ordinary World** must be identified and named.

## A. Identification of Forces

### Common Forces
- Lack of self-confidence
- Worrying about others' opinions
- Doubting abilities
- Bad attitude
- Fear of failure
- Procrastination

### Hidden Forces

#### Imposter Syndrome
- **Symptoms:** Dismissing success as luck, fearing being "found out"
- **Narrative:** "I don't deserve this. They'll realize I'm a fraud."
- **Countermeasure:** Keep a "Win Log" of undeniable achievements

#### Personal Trauma
- **Symptoms:** Past emotional scars used to narrate a story of limitation
- **Narrative:** "Because of [past event], I can never [desired outcome]."
- **Countermeasure:** Separate the event from your permanent identity

#### Tribal Narratives
- **Symptoms:** Unconscious stories adopted from one's "tribe"
- **Examples:**
  - "Our family isn't meant for wealth."
  - "We're not the type to speak up."
  - "People like us don't do that."
- **Countermeasure:** Question whether the narrative serves your current goals

## B. The Naming Technique

Analysts must drag the Enemy from the shadows by giving it a specific name and form to create psychological distance.

### Guidelines for Naming:

1. **Make it Non-Threatening (Cute/Small):**
   - "Beagle" — the hyper-distracted puppy
   - "Suzie" — the inner critic
   - "Igor Jr." — the small, manageable version of a big fear

2. **Make it a Fierce Opponent:**
   - "Igor" — the bully
   - "The Saboteur"
   - "The Gatekeeper"

3. **The Goal:** The name creates **psychological distance** so the individual can fight the Enemy objectively instead of internalizing the limitation.

### Naming Exercise:
- "If this limiting force were a character, what would it be?"
- "What would you call the part of you that holds you back?"
- "If this fear were a person, what would their name be?"

## C. Diagnostic Questions

Use these to probe each layer:

1. **What is the Field of Play where you feel stuck?**
2. **What does the 'Enemy' say to you in that moment?**
3. **When did this voice first appear?**
4. **What is the 'tribal narrative' you've been carrying?**
5. **If the Enemy had a name, what would it be?**
6. **What would you do if you weren't afraid?**

## D. The Five Bridges to Progress

Evaluate the shift from Ordinary to Extraordinary:

| Ordinary World | Extraordinary World |
| :--- | :--- |
| **STOP** | **START** |
| What to stop doing | What to start doing |
| **LESS OF** | **CONTINUE** |
| What to experience less of | What to continue doing |
| | **MORE OF** |
| | What to experience more of |

## E. The Five Whys

Keep asking "Why?" until reaching a deep emotional core:

- "Why do I want this goal?"
- "Why does that matter?"
- "Why is that important?"
- "Why does that resonate?"
- "Why is that the ultimate truth?"
