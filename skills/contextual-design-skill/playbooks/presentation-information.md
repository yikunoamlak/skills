# Presentation & Information Design Context Playbook

## Context ladder
`element → slide/page → presentation/story → meeting/event → decision context`

## Ask
- Is the audience listening, reading, or both?
- Will the speaker control pacing?
- What decision must the audience make?
- What can be shown versus spoken?
- What comes before and after the slide?

## Recommendations
- Design the narrative sequence, not isolated slides.
- Use one primary message per view when possible.
- Let visuals reduce explanation load.
- Make charts legible at presentation distance.
- Test on the actual display and room size.
