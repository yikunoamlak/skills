# Graphic Design Context Playbook

## Context ladder
`element → composition → series/campaign → channel/environment → audience/culture`

## Ask
- Is this viewed in isolation or beside other communications?
- At what distance and duration?
- Is it printed, projected, on-screen, or posted physically?
- What must be understood before anything is read?
- What does the surrounding visual field look like?
- Is the piece one-off or part of a repeatable system?

## Recommendations
- Design hierarchy before decoration.
- Establish viewing distance and first-glance priority.
- Design cropping and scale variations.
- Test monochrome and reproduction constraints where relevant.
- For series, define the constant and variable parts.
- Use proximity, similarity, continuation, figure-ground, and common region intentionally.
- Preserve enough distinctive context that the piece works as communication, not just as an attractive composition.
