# Product & Packaging Context Playbook

## Context ladder
`part/material → product/package → use/opening task → shelf/distribution → service/lifecycle/environment`

## Ask
- How is it held, opened, stored, transported, displayed, used, cleaned, repaired, or discarded?
- What other objects surround it?
- What does the user need to recognize before touching it?
- What manufacturing tolerances and materials are real?
- How does packaging affect the first-use experience?

## Recommendations
- Design touch, grip, force, sequence, and feedback.
- Treat manufacturing and assembly as design inputs.
- Test shelf distance and clutter.
- Consider lifecycle, repair, reuse, recycling, and disposal.
- Ensure the visual identity survives real material and printing conditions.
