# Art & Illustration Context Playbook

## Context ladder
`mark/form → artwork → series/body of work → presentation/publication → audience/culture`

## Ask
- Why does this work exist in this body of work?
- What visual language is established elsewhere?
- How long and from what distance is it viewed?
- Is it reproduced, printed, animated, exhibited, or screen-based?
- Which cultural or historical references are activated?

## Recommendations
- Let context influence composition and symbolism.
- Test work both alone and beside related work.
- Consider reproduction as a transformation, not a neutral copy.
- Distinguish intentional ambiguity from accidental confusion.
