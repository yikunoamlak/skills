# Architecture & Spatial Context Playbook

## Context ladder
`detail → room → building → site → neighborhood → landscape/city`

## Ask
- How does the person approach and enter?
- What are the circulation paths and thresholds?
- What do light, heat, wind, sound, topography, views, and materials demand?
- Which local patterns, crafts, histories, or social practices matter?
- How does the building change the public realm?
- How will it age and adapt?

## Recommendations
- Resolve site before facade decoration.
- Use local conditions as design inputs, not ornamental motifs.
- Test human scale and movement.
- Think about public/private gradients and orientation.
- Evaluate building, landscape, and street together.
