# Environmental / Exhibition Context Playbook

## Context ladder
`object/label → display → room → visitor route → institution → place/culture`

## Ask
- Where does the visitor arrive from?
- What does the visitor see first?
- How long do they have?
- How does movement create sequence and hierarchy?
- What are the light, acoustics, accessibility, crowding, and wayfinding conditions?

## Recommendations
- Design the route, not just displays.
- Use thresholds and orientation cues.
- Account for standing/sitting height and viewing distance.
- Coordinate graphics, architecture, artifacts, lighting, sound, and wayfinding as one environment.
