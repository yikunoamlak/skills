# Video / Film / Motion Context Playbook

## Context ladder
`frame → shot → scene → sequence → story → release/platform/audience`

## Ask
- What does the previous shot establish?
- Where should attention enter and exit?
- What is the emotional and narrative function of the shot?
- How will sound, dialogue, music, subtitles, and motion interact?
- Is this watched on phone, TV, cinema, social feed, presentation, or signage?
- What is the aspect ratio and safe-area context?

## Recommendations
- Evaluate motion as part of a sequence.
- Use staging to control attention.
- Use continuity where spatial/temporal comprehension matters.
- Use common fate and coordinated motion to show relationships.
- Do not animate simply because a still frame feels boring.
- Test full playback, not single frames.
- Check readability without perfect viewing conditions.
