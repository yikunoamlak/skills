# Subject Catalog — Afewerk Tekle Painting Style

## How to Render Specific Elements

This catalog describes how to translate any subject into Afewerk Tekle's monumental, luminous oil painting language.

---

## Human Figures

### Face — Classical Realism
- **Shape**: Oval, slightly elongated, sculpted features
- **Eyes**: Large, soulful, deeply expressive with defined eyelids
- **Nose**: Strong, defined bridge, sculpted
- **Mouth**: Full lips, often slightly parted or in gentle curve
- **Cheekbones**: High, defined, catching light
- **Jawline**: Strong, sculpted
- **Expression**: Dignified, contemplative, or heroic — never casual

### Face — Cubist Phase
- Same underlying structure as classical
- Fractured by intersecting dark geometric lines
- Features visible through the fractures
- Colors remain vibrant within fragments
- Stained-glass effect

### Neck (Signature Feature)
- **Elongated**: Slightly longer than classical proportion
- **Graceful**: Smooth, elegant line
- **Function**: Adds monumentality and elegance
- **Always visible**: Even in draped garments, the neck is suggested

### Shoulders & Torso
- **Broad, strong shoulders**: Dignified posture
- **Upright torso**: Never slouching, never casual
- **Volumetric**: Clear anatomical structure under clothing
- **Statuesque**: Like a classical sculpture

### Hands
- **Elegant**: Refined, expressive
- **Deliberate posing**: Holding objects, gesturing, resting
- **Defined fingers**: Not hyper-detailed, but clearly structured
- **Expressive**: Convey meaning through gesture

### Hair
- **Style**: Natural Ethiopian styles — short curls, braids, wrapped
- **Texture**: Indicated through brushwork, not individual strands
- **Color**: Deep mahogany to black
- **Volume**: Full, with light catching the crown

## Garments

### Habesha Kemis (White Dress)
```
┌──────────────────────┐
│ ╔══════════════════╗ │  ← Colorful tilet neckline
│ ║                  ║ │
│ ║   LUMINOUS       ║ │  ← White fabric with volumetric folds
│ ║   WHITE          ║ │     Light catches on raised folds
│ ║   COTTON         ║ │     Warm shadows in recesses
│ ║                  ║ │
│ ╠══════════════════╣ │  ← Colorful tilet waistband
│ ║                  ║ │
│ ║   WHITE          ║ │
│ ║   SKIRT          ║ │  ← Heavy, structured folds
│ ║                  ║ │
│ ╠══════════════════╣ │  ← Colorful tilet hem border
└──────────────────────┘
```
- **Base**: Luminous titanium white
- **Folds**: Heavy, volumetric, architectural
- **Light**: Catches on raised folds — brightest element
- **Shadows**: Warm cream, light amber — never gray
- **Tilet**: Geometric woven borders at neckline, waist, hem, sleeves

### Netela (Shawl)
```
     ┌───────────┐
    ╱   TILET     ╲   ← Colorful border
   ╱    BORDER     ╲
  ╱                 ╲
 ╱   LUMINOUS        ╲  ← White fabric with folds
╱     WHITE SHAWL      ╲
╲                     ╱
 ╲                   ╱
  ╲─────────────────╱
```
- **Draping**: Over shoulders, may cover head
- **Border**: Colorful tilet along all edges
- **Folds**: Heavy, structured, catching light
- **Glow**: The white fabric radiates light

### Tilet (Woven Border) — Critical Detail
```
┌─────────────────────────┐
│ ◆ ■ ◆ ■ ◆ ■ ◆ ■ ◆ ■ ◆ │  ← Diamond pattern
│ ■ ◆ ■ ◆ ■ ◆ ■ ◆ ■ ◆ ■ │     Alternating colors:
│ ◆ ■ ◆ ■ ◆ ■ ◆ ■ ◆ ■ ◆ │     red, gold, green, black
└─────────────────────────┘
```
- **Pattern**: Geometric — diamonds, zigzags, crosses, squares
- **Colors**: Deep red, emerald green, gold, black, sapphire blue
- **Placement**: On white garments, defining edges and identity
- **Detail**: Meticulously rendered — this is the cultural anchor
- **Width**: Typically 2-4 inches on actual garments

### Ceremonial Robes (Clergy/Royalty)
- **Colors**: Crimson, deep blue, gold, purple
- **Texture**: Heavy, embroidered, ornate
- **Gold embroidery**: Intricate patterns in gold thread
- **Layering**: Multiple garments visible
- **Accessories**: Crosses, medallions, sashes

## Objects

### Ethiopian Cross
- **Style**: Elaborate, interlocking designs
- **Material**: Gold, silver, or bronze
- **Detail**: Geometric patterns within the cross form
- **Usage**: Held by clergy, worn as pendant, architectural element
- **Varieties**: Lalibela, Gondar, Axumite, processional

### Processional Cross
- **Form**: Cross on a tall pole/stand
- **Material**: Gold or silver, ornate
- **Usage**: Carried in ceremonies
- **Detail**: Intricate metalwork, sometimes with banner

### Ceremonial Umbrella
- **Shape**: Domed top on pole
- **Colors**: Concentric rings of bright colors
- **Tassels**: Hanging from edges
- **Usage**: Over clergy, sacred objects, royalty
- **Symbol**: Protection, honor, sacredness

### Jebena (Coffee Pot)
- **Shape**: Round body, narrow neck, curved spout
- **Material**: Black clay
- **Detail**: Simple, elegant form
- **Usage**: Coffee ceremonies, domestic scenes

### Cotton/Spinning
- **Raw cotton**: White, fluffy cloud-like form
- **Thread**: Thin, delicate line
- **Spindle**: Wooden tool
- **Usage**: Allegorical scenes of industry

### Musical Instruments
- **Masinqo**: One-stringed fiddle
- **Kebero**: Drum
- **Washint**: Flute
- **Style**: Rendered with classical detail, not simplified

## Nature

### Trees
- **Acacia**: Flat-topped, umbrella shape, iconic Ethiopian form
- **Fig**: Round canopy, dense foliage
- **Palm**: Tall trunk, frond crown
- **Style**: Idealized, not photorealistic — luminous greens, warm light

### Mountains
- **Style**: Layered, receding into distance
- **Colors**: Green in foreground, blue in distance
- **Form**: Rounded peaks, not jagged
- **Function**: Create depth and Ethiopian sense of place

### Water
- **Lakes**: Calm, reflective, blue
- **Style**: Luminous, not photorealistic
- **Function**: Serene background element

### Sky
- **Style**: Warm, luminous, idealized
- **Colors**: Amber near horizon, sapphire above
- **Clouds**: Soft, luminous, not dramatic
- **Function**: Creates atmosphere and light

## Architecture

### Church Interior
- **Elements**: Arches, columns, curtains, altar
- **Style**: Monumental, sacred, ornate
- **Colors**: Gold, crimson, deep green
- **Light**: Ethereal, divine, coming from above

### Palatial Spaces
- **Elements**: Columns, arches, rich curtains
- **Style**: Grand, dignified
- **Colors**: Deep reds, golds, greens
- **Function**: Frame for monumental figures

### Traditional Ethiopian
- **Tukul**: Round hut, conical roof
- **Style**: Idealized, not rustic
- **Function**: Landscape element, cultural marker

## Stained Glass Elements

### Lead Lines
- **Color**: Dark brown to black
- **Width**: Thick, deliberate
- **Pattern**: Intersecting geometric lines
- **Function**: Fractures composition into color fields

### Glass Colors
- **Same as oil palette**: Emerald, crimson, gold, sapphire
- **Effect**: Luminous, light streaming through
- **Texture**: Slightly varied within each fragment

### Composition Fracturing
- **Lines follow form**: They respect the figure's structure
- **Not random**: Deliberate geometric organization
- **Classical underneath**: Realism remains visible
- **Stained-glass effect**: Like a cathedral window
