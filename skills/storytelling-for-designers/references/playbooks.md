# Playbooks - What the user wants to tell -> which principles

Match the user's own words to one playbook. Load the spine first, then only the category files that hold the "add when" entries you will actually use. Playbooks overlap; pick one primary and borrow from a second if the brief spans both.

Numbers refer to `principles-index.md`.

---

### 1. Pitch, proposal, or buy-in
**Hear:** pitch deck, investor update, get approval, stakeholder buy-in, sell this idea, grant proposal
**Spine:** 88 Trigger or Wicked Problem -> 11 Conflict -> 83 Hero's Journey (client as hero) -> 93 Three-Act Structure -> 73 Show, Don't Tell -> 7 Calls to Action
**Add when:**
- numbers are contested: 15 Data Storytelling, 17 Deductions, 86 The Peak-End Rule
- audience is skeptical or bored: 57 Narrative Transportation, 96 Verisimilitude, 98 Vulnerability
- it competes on features: 91 The Quarter-Inch Hole, 41 Jobs to Be Done, 42 Kano and Storytelling
- cross-cultural room: 35 Hofstede's Cultural Dimensions, 47 Maslow's Hierarchy of Need, 26 Ethics
**Hand back:** a one-sentence message, the arc mapped slide by slide, the three highest-impact edits, exact CTA wording.
**Watch:** making yourself or your product the hero (83); a MacGuffin nobody cares about (84).

### 2. Explain something complex
**Hear:** simplify this, explain our technology, make this understandable, technical content, onboarding copy for experts
**Spine:** 3 Analogy -> 51 Metaphors -> 79 Synaptic Pruning -> 69 Rule of Three -> 27 Exposition -> 73 Show, Don't Tell
**Add when:**
- the concept is invisible or abstract: 46 Mary's Room, 50 Mentalese, 3 Analogy
- it must feel true, not dumbed down: 96 Verisimilitude, 17 Deductions, 26 Ethics
- it needs a memorable shape: 44 Kishotenketsu, 25 Essence Model, 45 Lexicon
- stakes must be felt: 88 The Trigger or Wicked Problem, 87 The Senses
**Hand back:** an analogy ladder (concrete -> abstract), a 3-beat explanation, the jargon to cut, one worked example.
**Watch:** exposition dumps (27); metaphors that break under scrutiny.

### 3. Data, results, and reporting
**Hear:** dashboard, annual report, board deck, analytics, metrics, make the numbers land
**Spine:** 15 Data Storytelling -> 70 Sensemaking -> 73 Show, Don't Tell -> 86 The Peak-End Rule -> 7 Calls to Action
**Add when:**
- the audience must decide: 17 Deductions, 99 Work Backwards, 41 Jobs to Be Done
- the story spans time: 30 Freytag's Pyramid, 12 Contrast, Beginning to End, 97 Vonnegut's Shapes of Stories
- trust is fragile: 26 Ethics, 96 Verisimilitude, 49 Meaning versus Value
**Hand back:** the one question the data answers, the annotated exhibit, the before/after contrast, the action line under the chart.
**Watch:** chart junk and decorative series; numbers with no protagonist or consequence.

### 4. Brand identity and positioning
**Hear:** brand story, rebrand, logo story, visual identity, positioning, voice and tone, naming
**Spine:** 25 Essence Model -> 13 Core Values -> 5 Archetypes -> 94 Tone and Tenor -> 55 Narrative Design -> 78 Super Normal
**Add when:**
- the brand must earn belief: 6 Aspiration, 53 Mission/Vision/Value Statements, 26 Ethics
- it lives among lookalikes: 12 Contrast, Beginning to End, 67 Resource-Based Theory, 91 The Quarter-Inch Hole
- words carry the brand: 45 Lexicon, 51 Metaphors, 80 Synecdoche
- it must travel across cultures: 35 Hofstede's Cultural Dimensions, 100 World-Building
**Hand back:** the brand in one sentence (essence), the archetype and its shadow, 3 voice rules with do/don't lines, what to leave out.
**Watch:** values nobody can see in the work; a personality that contradicts the product.

### 6. Cause, impact, and advocacy
**Hear:** campaign, raise awareness, get people to act, nonprofit, petition, social impact, fundraiser
**Spine:** 88 Trigger or Wicked Problem -> 11 Conflict -> 23 Empathy -> 6 Aspiration -> 7 Calls to Action
**Add when:**
- the audience is distant from the problem: 60 Personas, 9 Character Relatability, 57 Narrative Transportation
- compassion fatigue is likely: 98 Vulnerability, 16 Deceptive Cadence, 37 Hyperbole (use sparingly)
- behaviour must change: 76 S-O-R Theory, 47 Maslow's Hierarchy of Need, 75 Stickiness
- credibility is at risk: 26 Ethics, 96 Verisimilitude, 15 Data Storytelling
**Hand back:** a real protagonist, the stakes in one line, the ask, and the smallest next step for the audience.
**Watch:** abstract statistics with no face; asking for everything at once (7).

### 9. Service and experience journey
**Hear:** customer journey, service design, onboarding flow, event, retail, exhibition, end-to-end experience

### 12. Portfolio and case studies

### 13. Review or critique an existing design
**Hear:** review this, critique my page, why is this not working, feedback on my deck, audit

### 15. Internal alignment and culture
**Hear:** mission, values, culture deck, change programme, internal launch, align the team
**Spine:** 53 Mission/Vision/Value Statements -> 13 Core Values -> 8 Ceremonies and Rituals -> 92 Thesis + Antithesis = Synthesis -> 6 Aspiration
**Add when:**

- leadership must model it: 23 Empathy, 98 Vulnerability, 26 Ethics
**Hand back:** the change as a before/after story, three observable behaviours per value, a ritual that reinforces it.
**Watch:** abstract value words with no behaviour attached; a vision only leadership can recite.

---

## Choosing between playbooks

1. Take the playbook whose **hand back** line matches what the user asked to receive.
2. If the brief spans two, use one as the spine and borrow at most three entries from the other.
3. If nothing fits, the need is unusual: scan `principles-index.md` instead and build a custom set.
4. Never return more than 15 principles total. Depth on a few beats coverage of many.
